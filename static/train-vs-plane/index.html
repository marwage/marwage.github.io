Assumptions

  • Train speed: 250 km/h. This is the ICE's top speed, not its average. Real average speeds are lower, especially on routes with stops.
  • Plane speed: 830 km/h. Typical cruise speed of an Airbus A320neo (Mach 0.78). Max cruise is about 870 km/h; the separate flight-overhead field covers taxi, takeoff, climb, and descent.
  • Door-to-door, one way. Access at both ends and buffers are included. Direct trips only; no transfers or flight connections.
  • Distance is city-to-city. Actual rail and air routes may be longer.
  • Not modeled: ticket cost, CO2 emissions, schedule frequency, time of day, and time zones.

How it works

Each mode has a fixed time overhead and a distance-dependent travel time:

F_train = to station + buffer + from station F_plane = to airport + buffer + flight overhead + from airport T_train = F_train + 60 × d / 250 T_plane = F_plane + 60 × d / 830 break-even d* = (F_plane − F_train) / (60/250 − 60/830)

All values are in minutes and kilometres. The break-even is the distance where the two lines cross: below it the train is faster, above it the plane is faster.
